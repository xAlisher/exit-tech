## Growth mechanics

### Shareable exit card
Generated on every exit post. Dark, stark, strikethrough, your handle.
Looks different from everything else in a feed — that's how it spreads.

### Exit anniversaries
"3 years since you exited Facebook" — auto reminder, reshare moment.
Like a sobriety anniversary. Emotionally charged, highly shareable.

### Exit count flex
"I've exited 23 dependencies" — natural brag in this community.

### Farcaster / Nostr native
Target audience lives there. Frame for logging without leaving feed.

### Challenges
Monthly community exits. Coordinated posting waves.

---

## North star
Exit.tech is not a product. It's a mirror — showing people what they've escaped,
who escaped with them, and what's possible next.
The goal isn't growth. It's awakening.

Exit Google Photos

Your memories, their training data, rented back to you monthly.

// why

  • Storage rent that only goes up — your library grows, so does the bill.
  • Photos are scanned and analyzed; lock-in deepens with every album.
  • Terms of service rated “E” — see the documented points at ToS;DR

// first, get your stuff out

  1. Google Takeout — takeout.google.com → deselect all → Google Photos → export. Expect big archives; originals + metadata in JSON sidecars.
  2. Data export links at JustGetMyData

// exit paths

[self-host] Self-host your library

Immich

The Google Photos experience, on your own hardware. immich-go imports Takeout archives directly.

via awesome-selfhosted
PhotoPrism

AI-powered library with strong search; single Docker container.

via awesome-selfhosted

[switch] Switch to an encrypted service

Ente Photos

End-to-end encrypted, open source, imports from Takeout. Paying with money instead of data.

via privacy-guides

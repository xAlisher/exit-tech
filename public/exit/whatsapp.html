Exit WhatsApp

Meta reads the metadata of everyone you love.

// why

  • Owned by Meta — contact graph and metadata feed the same ad machine as Facebook and Instagram.
  • Phone number required, contacts uploaded, message metadata retained.
  • Terms of service rated “C” — see the documented points at ToS;DR

// first, get your stuff out

  1. Export your chats — Settings → Chats → Export chat (per conversation, with or without media).
  2. Request your account info — Settings → Account → Request account info.
  3. Data export links at JustGetMyData

// exit paths

[switch] Switch to a private messenger

Signal open source

The default answer. Same UX as WhatsApp, E2EE by default, nonprofit.

via awesome-privacy privacy-guides switching-software
SimpleX Chat open source

No identifiers at all — not even a phone number.

via awesome-privacy privacy-guides
Session open source

Decentralized, no phone number, onion-routed.

via web3privacy awesome-privacy

// burn the bridge

Delete your account (difficulty: medium) — direct link via JustDeleteMe

// take an agent with you

Paste this into your agent* of choice and it will walk you through the exit, personalized:

I want to exit WhatsApp. Act as my exit guide.

First, help me get my data out:
- Export your chats — Settings → Chats → Export chat (per conversation, with or without media).
- Request your account info — Settings → Account → Request account info.

Switch to a private messenger: help me choose between Signal, SimpleX Chat, Session based on my situation — ask me what matters before recommending.
When I'm ready, walk me through deleting my account (difficulty: medium): https://www.whatsapp.com/faq/search/?q=how%20do%20I%20delete%20my%20account?&p=desktop

The hardest part of leaving WhatsApp is the people. Help me write a short, non-preachy message to my groups explaining where I went.
Help me decide between Signal, SimpleX and Session based on who I actually talk to.

Go step by step. One thing at a time. I want to actually finish this.

* Just a reminder — you can also exit ⎋Claude, ⎋ChatGPT or ⎋Gemini in favor of locally hosted agents.

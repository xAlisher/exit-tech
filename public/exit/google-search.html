Exit Google Search

The answer's still there — under the ads, the AI box, and the SEO sludge.

// why

  • Every search is logged to your profile by default; the query box is also the most honest record of what you think about.
  • The results decay toward whatever's monetized — ads and SEO-gamed pages sit above what you actually asked for.
  • Terms of service rated “E” — see the documented points at ToS;DR

// first, get your stuff out

  1. Review and delete your past searches at myactivity.google.com (filter to Search) — or pull the full history via takeout.google.com first.
  2. Turn off collection going forward — myactivity.google.com → Web & App Activity → Turn off, and set auto-delete.

// exit paths

[switch] Switch your default engine

DuckDuckGo open source

The easiest switch — no tracking, results good enough for most days. The default landing if you just want out without thinking about it.

via privacy-guides awesome-privacy
Startpage

Google's actual results, proxied so Google never sees you — the pick if you switched before and missed the result quality.

via privacy-guides awesome-privacy
Brave Search open source · audited

Runs on its own independent index, not Bing or Google — the most genuinely de-Googled option, though its index is younger and occasionally thinner.

via privacy-guides awesome-privacy
Mojeek

A truly independent crawler with no Big-Tech backend. Best when independence is the whole point; expect to fall back to another engine for long-tail queries.

via awesome-privacy

// take an agent with you

Paste this into your agent* of choice and it will walk you through the exit, personalized:

I want to exit Google Search. Act as my exit guide.

First, help me get my data out:
- Review and delete your past searches at myactivity.google.com (filter to Search) — or pull the full history via takeout.google.com first.
- Turn off collection going forward — myactivity.google.com → Web & App Activity → Turn off, and set auto-delete.

Switch your default engine: help me choose between DuckDuckGo, Startpage, Brave Search, Mojeek based on my situation — ask me what matters before recommending.

Before we pick an engine, interview me: is this my browser's default search, do I need strong image/video results, and how much result quality am I willing to trade for privacy?
Switching engines doesn't erase my past data — help me review and delete my Google search history first.
Set me a one-week "search diet": try answering from direct knowledge, Wikipedia, or a specific site's own search before reaching for a general engine — so I'm leaving the habit, not just the brand.

Go step by step. One thing at a time. I want to actually finish this.

* Just a reminder — you can also exit ⎋Claude, ⎋ChatGPT or ⎋Gemini in favor of locally hosted agents.

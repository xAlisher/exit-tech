Exit Netflix

You don't own anything, and they know what makes you cry.

// why

  • Subscription model for content you can't keep; the catalog rotates beneath you and the algorithm optimizes for retention, not satisfaction.
  • Price hikes, password crackdowns, and autoplay engineered for one more episode.
  • Terms of service rated “D” — see the documented points at ToS;DR

// first, get your stuff out

  1. Download your viewing activity — Account → Viewing activity. Note: this export is often incomplete.
  2. Request your full data archive at netflix.com/account/getmyinfo — arrives by email as a zip.

// exit paths

[self-host] Own your media, stream it yourself

Jellyfin

A Netflix-grade interface for media you actually own. Free, open source, clients for every screen in the house.

via awesome-selfhosted

[switch] Change how you pay for stories

Your public library

Streaming (Kanopy, Hoopla) comes free with most library cards — curated by librarians, not engagement metrics.

Physical media

A used Blu-ray costs less than a month of streaming and can't be pulled from your shelf.

// burn the bridge

Delete your account (difficulty: impossible) — direct link via JustDeleteMe

Go step by step. One thing at a time. I want to actually finish this.
